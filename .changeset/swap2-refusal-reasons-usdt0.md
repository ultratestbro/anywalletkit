Refusal reasons. USDT0's refusals keep their text and now carry a `reason` (`Swap2RefusalReason`), as `onRefusal`'s second argument and on the thrown errors (still plain `Error`s with the same message). In `getQuote`, for OFT sends and the Legacy Mesh alike: flexible amounts, a chain without USDT0 or a mesh OFT, the same chain, another token, no RPC for the source and an OFT asking for an LZ-token fee are `unsupported_pair`; a zero amount, the OFT minimum and dust are `amount_too_small`; the OFT maximum is `amount_too_large` on an OFT send and `insufficient_liquidity` on the Legacy Mesh (it is the pool's credit toward Tron / TON), as is a `quoteSend` reverting with `InsufficientCredits`; the caller's floor above the output and an OFT reporting more received than sent are `price_check_failed`; a recipient or refund address the destination cannot pay (another family, a TON workchain other than 0, a testnet address) and the zero account are `recipient_mismatch`; a live quote without its recipient or refund address is `policy_refused`. A token or approval mode the chain reports against the pins, or a peer that is another contract, is `vault_mismatch`; a peer that is not wired at all (zero) is `unsupported_pair`. The source RPC's own failure keeps its fixed text ("RPC for … unavailable — try again") and is classified from viem's error: 429 `rate_limited` with `retryAfterMs` from Retry-After, a node's limit-exceeded answer or a 429 code in its JSON-RPC answer `rate_limited`, 401 / 403 `key_rejected`, any other status (a failing status that comes with a JSON-RPC body is read the same way), a network failure or viem's timeout `upstream_unavailable` (with `httpStatus` when there was one); any other revert of the OFT's read is `simulation_failed`. In `preflightDeposit` (both routes), another deposit target is `vault_mismatch`, a refund to another address and the zero recipient are `recipient_mismatch`, a LayerZero fee that rose past the sealed headroom is `quote_expired`, a placeholder-priced quote, a malformed intent id, missing sealed parameters and any calldata, fee, value or chain off the sealed send are `policy_refused`, and a re-thrown RPC failure keeps the inner classification (a lost peer stays `unsupported_pair`, a rejected key `key_rejected`). `buildDeposit` on a placeholder-priced quote or one without a transaction is `policy_refused`. LayerZero Scan's answers that reach `buildClaim` carry their status (429 `rate_limited` with Retry-After, 401 / 403 `key_rejected`, any other `upstream_unavailable` with `httpStatus`); a network failure, the module's own timeout or a body that is not JSON is the same error tagged `upstream_unavailable`; an incomplete stored packet is `upstream_unavailable` and one stored for another receiver `vault_mismatch`. `usdt0Pins.reasonOf` classifies every text `usdt0VerifyQuote` (and so `usdt0VerifyMeshSend`) returns, so a remote stub's refusal carries it: a route or token this venue does not serve `unsupported_pair`; another recipient, refund address or destination eid, the zero account, or a transaction on a placeholder-priced quote `recipient_mismatch`; another OFT, spender or a deposit address `vault_mismatch`; a minimum below the floor or more out than in `price_check_failed`; amounts that are not integers `upstream_unavailable`; any other call shape, amount, fee or intent `policy_refused`. A quote whose `sourceAmount` is not an integer still makes the check throw; that error's text is classified `upstream_unavailable` the same way. The provider's self-check of its own Legacy Mesh build reports the check's reason. Configuration errors and a non-integer `amountIn` stay unclassified.
