v3 and v4 quotes carry `gasEstimate` (`gasEstimateSource: "simulated"`): the QuoterV2 / V4Quoter `gasEstimate` the quote already reads (no extra call) plus a measured per-flow overhead for what the transaction spends around the pools (intrinsic gas, calldata, router, input payment, payout; v4 SETTLE/TAKE and the SwapProxy). With `venueRoutes: "both"` each venue's row carries its own figure. Set only where the quote has a gas limit, on chains whose schedule was measured (v3: 1, 10, 56, 130, 143, 196, 480, 8453, 42161, 57073, 59144; v4: those plus 5042 and 42220), and never above the limit; Polygon, Robinhood Chain, Celo v3, zkSync, MegaETH, Avalanche, Elysium and HyperEVM get none. Monad's table is calibrated against `eth_estimateGas` (Monad charges the limit). Accuracy (README): v3 median 4.7 %, v4 4.3 % on 157 simulated quotes; 378 real SwapRouter02 receipts median 4.6 %, 90 % within 12.2 %. RPC budgets, pins, the impact gate, gas bounds and `providerData` are unchanged; `tests/unset-byte-identical.test.ts` compares every other quote field with 0.4.11 as before and checks the two new fields against the core contract.
