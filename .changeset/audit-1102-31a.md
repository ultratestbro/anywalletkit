A Mayan bridge order out of Solana is now always held to a reviewed token and minimum.

- `protocol-swap2-core`: `Quote` gains an optional `destDecimals` (the destination token's decimals as the provider reports them). The engine's `bridgeOut.destDecimals` uses the asset catalog's decimals and, for a destination token the catalog does not list, the quote's — before, such a token left `bridgeOut` without decimals and the order's minimum unchecked. The swap2 server seals `destDecimals` (a whole number in 0..36; anything else is refused like any malformed quote field).
- `protocol-swap2-lifi`: quotes carry `destDecimals` from LI.FI's `action.toToken.decimals`.
- `wallet-solana`: with `expect.bridgeOut`, a Mayan order is refused when the guard names no `destToken` (`bridge-token`) or no `minAmountOut` with `destDecimals` (`bridge-min`), instead of passing with only its own non-zero minimum.

What to do: nothing when you map the engine's expectation with `serializedGuardFromExecution`. A hand-built `bridgeOut` for a Mayan route must now carry `destToken`, `minAmountOut` and `destDecimals`. A custom provider that serves unlisted destination tokens through Mayan should fill `destDecimals` on its quotes, or those legs are refused at signing.
