Refusal reasons; every text is unchanged. Up front, a chain or token 1Click does not serve or list and an intents destination off NEAR are `unsupported_pair`, a corridor the app paused (`suspendedChains`) is `policy_refused` (the app's own switch, like a disabled provider), and an intents recipient no key controls is `recipient_mismatch`. 1Click's answers: a known amount floor is `amount_too_small` (its presentable text unchanged, so the swap2 server's `{noQuote}` now carries `reason: "amount_too_small"` next to it), 429 `rate_limited` with Retry-After, 401 / 403 `key_rejected`, 408 / 425 / 5xx after the retries `upstream_unavailable` with the status unless the message names a passing condition (liquidity, a rate limit with its wait, a halt), never a pair or route verdict, a network failure after the retries `upstream_unavailable`; on any other status "No liquidity" is `insufficient_liquidity`, other messages go by their meaning (`amount_too_large`, `recipient_mismatch`, `unsupported_pair`, `chain_halted`, `rate_limited` with Retry-After), anything else `no_route`; the caller's own abort is not classified. The quote checks: a live quote without a deposit address, a quote without a minimum or a deadline that does not parse are `upstream_unavailable`, a deadline already past `quote_expired`, another input `policy_refused`, a floor breach `price_check_failed`. `notifyDeposit` failures are classified by their HTTP status, or `upstream_unavailable` when 1Click is unreachable. A live quote without a refund address, a malformed intent id and configuration errors stay unclassified.
