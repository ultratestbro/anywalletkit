Refusal reasons. Jupiter's refusals keep their text and now carry a `reason` (`Swap2RefusalReason`): another chain, a flexible amount, an unknown or identical token, or a recipient other than the taker is `unsupported_pair`, a zero amount is `amount_too_small`, and the caller's slippage above the cap or a live quote without the signer's address is `policy_refused` (a signer address that is not a Solana address is the caller's argument and stays unclassified). A failed `/build` takes Jupiter's own error first (no route `no_route`, an untradable token `unsupported_pair`, a route that cannot take the whole amount `insufficient_liquidity`, amount limits) and otherwise its status (429 `rate_limited` with `retryAfterMs` from Retry-After or `x-ratelimit-reset`, capped at a day, 401 / 403 `key_rejected`, 408 / 425 / 5xx `upstream_unavailable` with `httpStatus`). A network failure, malformed JSON, an answer without an output amount or a blockhash, a swap instruction the decoder does not know, or lookup tables the transaction cannot be assembled with is `upstream_unavailable`; the caller's own abort (also while the body is read), an `apiUrl` that is not a URL and a default slippage above the cap stay unclassified. In the vetted instructions, the swap aimed at another program is `vault_mismatch`; a SOL transfer, account close, associated or initialized token account for someone else is `recipient_mismatch`; a quoted output, slippage or floor that fails is `price_check_failed`; and everything else the module never signs (another swap mode, mints or amount, a transaction version, a tip, a platform fee, foreign programs, extra signers, Token or System instructions the swap does not need) is `policy_refused`. A failing simulation is `simulation_failed`, and `buildDeposit` on a quote that is not live is `policy_refused`.
