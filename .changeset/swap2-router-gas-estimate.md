`evmSourceGas({..., useGasEstimate: true})` counts `Quote.gasEstimate` (the gas the source transaction is expected to use) when a quote has a usable one, clamped to [21,000, 3,000,000], instead of `txRequest.gasLimit` (capped) or the calldata / deposit-address defaults; `swapGasUnits(quote, params, {useGasEstimate})` likewise. Off by default: without the option the router ranks exactly as 0.8 did. Gasless quotes still count 0, and the ERC-20 approval (+50k unless the sender's allowance already covers the amount) is still added separately. With it, a venue whose limit carries headroom (Fynd: estimate × 1.3 + 80 000) is no longer charged for the headroom, and a quote without a transaction that states its gas (a dry KyberSwap quote, a 0x price) is charged that figure instead of a plain transfer. Venue figures are passed on as stated: 0x's and KyberSwap's run about a third above the gas used (protocol-swap2-core README, "Gas"). It does not settle a race smaller than the gas: the 2026-11-27 Ethereum Fynd 294.50 / CoW 294.49 case is a tie to a hundredth of a cent on Fynd's own estimate at that evening's gas price, and a CoW win on the gas the comparable recorded route used (README).

- RouteSet quotes, `getBestQuote` / `RoutedQuote`, remote stubs and `onUpdate` snapshots carry the two fields unchanged; `Swap2Route` gains `gasEstimate?` and `gasEstimateSource?` next to `gasUsd`. A quote with a broken estimate sits out with the rule named in `lastRefusals`.
- `allowanceReuse` and `revokeUnspentApproval` (protocol-swap2-core) are accepted on the router's engine config, survive `using(provider, routeId)`, and `swap2(options, {...})` overrides them for one call. The provider snapshot carries `exactAllowanceOnly`, so a provider that declares it (KyberSwap's generic-build routers) is held to the defaults through the router too.
- `evmSourceGas`'s RPC fetch uses core's `timeoutSignal` / `anySignal` instead of `AbortSignal.timeout` / `AbortSignal.any` (Hermes).
