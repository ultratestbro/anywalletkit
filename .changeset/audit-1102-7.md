A broadcast error that a gateway or load balancer gives when its upstream node failed — JSON-RPC `-32603` (internal error) or `-32005` (limit exceeded), or any code whose message says "upstream", "gateway", "timeout" or "unavailable", including a 5xx whose body is JSON-RPC shaped — now ends as `BroadcastUnknownError` (`maybeSent: true`) unless the message carries a node's exact refusal words ("nonce too low", "transaction underpriced", "insufficient funds for gas", …). Before, a loose word such as "nonce", "tip" or "fee cap" in such a message read as a definite refusal. Ordinary `-32000` node refusals are unchanged.
