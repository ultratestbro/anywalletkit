With `expect.bridgeOut`, a Mayan order's own minimum must now reach the reviewed `minAmountOut` less 1 bp of rounding. `minAmountOut` is the quote's minimum, already at the plan's slippage; the guard took `slippageBps` off it a second time, so an order could deliver up to that slippage (0.5 % at the default) under the minimum the user reviewed. `slippageBps` is still accepted and range-checked.
