A plain Mayan MCTP / Fast MCTP bridge out of Solana delivers Circle's native USDC, and with `expect.bridgeOut` the reviewed `destToken` must now be exactly that token on `destChain` (the addresses `@anywalletkit/protocol-swap2-cctp` uses; any letter case). Before, any reviewed 6-decimal token passed, so a review of USDbC, USDC.e or USDT could be met by a USDC bridge. A plain bridge towards a chain without CCTP (BSC) is refused (`bridge-token`). Mayan orders that name their own output token (Swift, the MCTP order modes) are unchanged. LI.FI's recorded MCTP and Fast MCTP routes pass. Nothing to change in apps.
