Solana Stake-program instructions are judged by whom they pay or empower, not by the program id: Withdraw names its recipient, Authorize* the new authority, Initialize* the staker, withdrawer and any custodian, SetLockup* the custodian, DelegateStake the vote account, Split / MoveStake / MoveLamports the destination account and Merge the account merged into. Allowlisting `Stake11111111111111111111111111111111111111` no longer lets a Withdraw or Authorize to a foreign address through: allowlist the wallet, the stake accounts and the vote accounts instead.
