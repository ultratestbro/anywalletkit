`KaminoMarketProtocolSolana.getPosition()` reads the wallet's obligations from the chain. Kamino's `/users/<owner>/obligations` now answers `deposits: {}` and `borrows: {}` and keeps the legs only in the row's raw `state`, so the position came back empty (a Lend balance of 0) for wallets with funds supplied, and the 1 % API-versus-chain check on a Lend `'max'` never ran. Now:

- `getPosition()` takes only the obligation addresses from the API, reads each obligation and the reserves it uses from the chain, and reports every leg at its reserve's on-chain rate (debt with the interest the reserve has accrued). Each obligation must be the wallet's on this market, and each reserve this market's, or the read is refused. The wallet's vanilla obligation is read even when the API leaves it out, and a listed obligation that no longer exists is dropped. The legs in `obligations[].deposits` / `.borrows` come from the chain and name their `reserve`. `valueUsd` is the API's figure where it gives one for that token, else `''`. LTVs stay the API's. The call now needs a `@anywalletkit/wallet-solana` account (`getAccountInfos`), like the writes already did.
- `KaminoApi.getUserObligations()` decodes the row's raw `state` into `ApiObligation.state`: cTokens per deposit reserve, and scaled debt with its borrow rate. A malformed figure is refused, not read as zero.
- A Lend `'max'` checks that `state` against the obligation on chain, valued at the reserves' on-chain rates, when the API gives no per-token legs. More than 1 % apart is refused before anything is built. The amount the guard enforces is still the chain's.

Apps that show a Kamino Lend balance should take this release. The README's Verify section also describes the repository's live check as it now runs: `verify:live [address] [rpcUrl] [lendAddress]` simulates a Lend withdraw for a wallet with USDC supplied on the main market and checks that wallet's `getPosition` against the chain. It stops with "holder's USDC account is frozen, pass another address" when the holder's USDC account is frozen, and skips only the deposits for a holder without USDC. Its default holder moved to an unfrozen USDC account.
