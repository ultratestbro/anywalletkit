Refusal reasons. Omniston's refusals keep their text and now carry a `reason` (`Swap2RefusalReason`): `getQuote` passes it to `onRefusal` as its second argument, and the errors thrown by `buildDeposit`, `trackIntent`, `registerSignedOrder`, `validateEvmTypedOrder` and `validateEvmToTonOrderExtension` carry it (`refusalOf`). At quote time a route that is not an order corridor, a chain without a trusted settlement contract or order protocol, missing validators, an uncatalogued token or a native leg is `unsupported_pair`, an amount above the per-swap cap `amount_too_large`, a destination or refund address of another chain `recipient_mismatch`, a settlement contract or order protocol outside the app's table `vault_mismatch`, a passed or too close trade-start deadline `quote_expired`, an output outside 0.5x–2x of the input `price_check_failed`, and a quote id the platform reused `policy_refused`. The RFQ: no resolver quoting after the venue acknowledged it is `no_route`; a stream that closed or stayed silent until the module's own window ran out, a stream error without the venue's own reason, a resolver update of another shape and an answer without order settlement are `upstream_unavailable`; an update with a stale timestamp is `quote_expired`. The caller's own abort still refuses nothing. Errors the Omniston SDK raises for the venue's calls (escrow, order payload, swap build, signed-order registration) and for the RFQ stream are classified from the reason the venue gave (`OmnistonError.details.error_info`): a quote the venue no longer holds is `quote_expired`, an unsupported route or operation and an unsupported token `unsupported_pair`, the swap limit `amount_too_large`, a wrong output amount or a slippage refund in the venue's emulation `price_check_failed`, any other emulation failure `simulation_failed`, an invalid argument unclassified, and anything else, or no structured reason, `upstream_unavailable`; a thrown error stays the same error, its class and text kept. At build time an expired quote, a pre-update intent id or a missing signed-order context is `quote_expired`; an intent id bound to another trader, recipient or owner `recipient_mismatch` (another kind or chain, or replaced quote terms, `policy_refused`); an untrusted settlement contract or extension target, an unconfirmed jetton wallet and a swap target or DEX leg the app does not recognize `vault_mismatch`; a body returning the excess or paying the output elsewhere and a signed TON address that is not the request's `recipient_mismatch`; an on-chain minimum under the quote `price_check_failed`; missing validators or a client without swap builds `unsupported_pair`; every other drift of the signed order, its extension, the order details or the TON message from the quote (malformed payloads, other amounts, unpinned profiles, more TON than the gas budget or the safety cap) `policy_refused`. `omnistonPins` gains `reasonOf` for the texts `omnistonVerifyBuild` returns, including `verifyTonEscrowBuild`'s, so a remote stub wired with `...omnistonPins(...)` carries the same reasons: an untrusted order protocol, escrow factory, protocol contract, DEX target or leg `vault_mismatch`, a `takingAmount` or delivery under the minimum and a decoded minimum under the quote `price_check_failed`, a receiver the extension does not whitelist and a signed TON address of another recipient `recipient_mismatch`, an unknown TON refund address `recipient_unverifiable`, an expiry already passed `quote_expired`, a missing hook, an unknown token or an uncheckable body kind `unsupported_pair`, and any other shape `policy_refused`. The notes behind `reasonOf` are one set per realm, so pins taken from the package root read the texts of an `omnistonVerifyBuild` taken from `./provider` in the CommonJS build too (each entry bundles its own copy there). Configuration errors, a malformed intent id or signature, an invalid amount and a missing refund address stay unclassified.
