`suggestTwoStep` (also `CrossChainRouter.suggestTwoStep`): when the auction has no route, price two ordinary swaps through a hub token (the native coin or main stablecoin of the source or destination chain) — step 2 on step 1's guaranteed output, a gas flag for the hub's chain, gas-free plans preferred within a band when the user lacks gas, the hub always delivered to the user's own address. Nothing is executed; `defaultHubs` lists the default hubs.
