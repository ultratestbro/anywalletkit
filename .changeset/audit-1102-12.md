`maxFeeRateMultiplier` is now validated when the signing account is created: it must be unset, `null`, `Infinity` or a number of at least 1. A value below 1, `NaN`, `-Infinity` or a non-number throws a `RangeError` (before, such values were accepted and refused fees at or near the network quote). A very large finite value (above 1,000,000, exported as `MAX_FEE_RATE_MULTIPLIER`) now means "off", like `Infinity`, instead of failing every signature with a `RangeError` from an overflow. The default (10) is unchanged.
