Refusal reasons. Chainflip's refusals keep their text and now carry a `reason` (`Swap2RefusalReason`): a chain without vault swaps, an unlisted asset or a quote type other than REGULAR is `unsupported_pair`, an amount below the minimum deposit `amount_too_small`, a floor above the expected output `price_check_failed`, a live quote without a refund address `policy_refused`. A failed backend answer takes the backend's own message first (limits, liquidity, a halted or disabled pool, no quote) and otherwise its status (429 `rate_limited` with `retryAfterMs` from Retry-After, 408 / 425 / 5xx `upstream_unavailable` with `httpStatus`); the backend takes no key, so a 401 / 403 (a CDN, WAF or region block) is `upstream_unavailable` with `httpStatus`, never `key_rejected`. A network failure, an answer of another shape (an encoder answer without a target among them) or an encoding that does not decode is `upstream_unavailable`, an empty quote list is `no_route`, and the caller's own abort, also while a body is read, stays unclassified. In the encoder's output, another vault or a TRC-20 transfer to someone else is `vault_mismatch`, another destination or refund address is `recipient_mismatch`, a Tron deposit without its note is `recipient_unverifiable`, a lower minimum price or another oracle guard is `price_check_failed`, and another token or amount, a broker or boost fee, DCA, affiliates or a long retry window is `policy_refused`. `buildDeposit` on a quote that is not a live vault swap is `policy_refused`. `chainflipPins.reasonOf` classifies every text `chainflipVerifyQuote` and the pins' `verifyBuild` return, so a remote stub's refusal carries the same reasons. An address or amount of the app's own that does not parse stays unclassified, in `getQuote` and in `chainflipVerifyQuote`.
