A write whose account signs on another network than the one it was asked for is refused before any rule, `confirm` or signature (`PolicyViolationError`, `policy: 'chain'`). With one `eip155` namespace registration whose RPC is on Ethereum, `getAccount('eip155:8453')` handed out the Ethereum account and `confirm` showed Base while the signature was for Ethereum; applying both networks' rules did not prevent it. `applyPolicies` now settles the network first — the account's own (asking `getChainId()` while it still reports `eip155:unknown`) and the `chainId` an EVM transaction carries — and compares; the rules of every network in play apply. Off-chain signatures need no RPC call: messages, and typed data, whose domain `chainId` stays the chain package's to judge (wallet-evm's `allowCrossChainTypedData`); an account with no provider at all (offline signing) is judged by the asked-for chain as before, while a provider that fails fails the write. `Wallet.getAccount` hands out such an account for reads (history and activity across one `eip155` registration keep working) but refuses its writes, with or without policies; it returns the same object for the same account and chain on every call. Short and genesis spellings of one network match (`solana:mainnet` = `solana:5eykt…`, `tron:728126428` = `tron:0x2b6653dc`); the new `sameChain()` is exported. Audit 2026-10-25, #7.
