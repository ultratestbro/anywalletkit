Inside a bridge out of Solana (`expect.bridgeOut`, Mayan routes), a Jupiter v1 route (`route`, `shared_accounts_route`) is now refused with `bridge-instruction`. Its `platform_fee_bps` sits after the variable-length route plan and was read as the data's last byte, so a trailing byte could hide a fee. Only the v2 routes, whose fee fields sit at fixed offsets, are followed; every recorded LI.FI Mayan route uses them and passes. Jupiter swaps outside a bridge are unchanged. Nothing to change in apps.
