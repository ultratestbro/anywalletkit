Inside a bridge out of Solana (`expect.bridgeOut`), a Jupiter route must now take no platform fee and no positive-slippage share (`platform_fee_bps` and `positive_slippage_bps` of `route_v2` / `shared_accounts_route_v2`, the u8 `platform_fee_bps` of the v1 routes); such a fee goes to an account the guard does not follow, so the wire is refused with `bridge-fee`, and a Jupiter route too short to read its fees with `bridge-instruction`. The route's slippage is not capped here — the bridge order's own minimum, checked against the reviewed one, bounds the price. LI.FI's live Mayan routes carry no Jupiter fee and pass unchanged. Nothing to change in apps.
