`maxPriceImpactBps` (opt-in): refuse a quote whose own price-impact measure (the larger of the 1/1000 probe and the spot value loss, at one block) is at or above the limit, as `insufficient_liquidity`; when the option is set and the spot measure cannot be read, refuse as `upstream_unavailable` (or `rate_limited`). Unset keeps today's behaviour (impact is information). A swap whose gas need exceeds the chain's per-transaction cap, block gas limit or (Arbitrum Nitro) execution cap is refused (it could never complete); a padded limit above the cap is clamped to it. Per-item RPC errors are no longer read as 'no pool'.

- With `maxPriceImpactBps` set, only the best-ranked route is gated (a refused quote never falls back to a lower-output candidate), every read is pinned to one block with transient RPC errors retried, and a probe too small to run, reverted or empty falls back to the spot measure. The spot measure itself must be read: pool lookups or `slot0()` reads unanswered after the retries, reverted, zero or in another shape refuse the quote (`rate_limited` / `upstream_unavailable`; `insufficient_liquidity` when the probe alone already reaches the limit) — the probe alone never passes a quote. The refusal text reads "loses 99.8% of the value to price impact — its pools are too thin for this amount (limit 5%; best route via USDC 0.3% → 0.01%)"; a passing quote adds `providerData.valueLossBps` (spot, fees included) next to the unchanged `priceImpactBps`. `preflightDeposit` does not apply it.
- With it unset, impact is never a refusal, and a healthy RPC sees 0.4.11's requests, sent as 0.4.11 sent them (every batch at once; concurrent quotes share no queue), checked scenario by scenario against the 0.4.11 dist, plus one `eth_getBlockByNumber` request per chain every 10 minutes. The gas bound and the RPC error handling below are always on and can refuse or clamp where 0.4.11 quoted.
- Gas: the need is the bare need (quoter gas + 120k + unwrap/refund) plus 5% for EIP-150's 63/64 call forwarding (live, swaps ran in 2.1–3.5% over the bare need). The per-transaction caps, read live on 2026-11-26 (`scripts/verify-live.mjs`: the `eth_estimateGas` allowance of an endless loop), are 16,777,216 (EIP-7825) on Ethereum, OP Mainnet, BNB Chain, Unichain, World Chain, Base, Ink and Linea, 33,554,432 on Polygon and 30M on Monad. On a chain without one, the block gas limit is the cap, less 1,000,000 on OP-stack chains (Celo, X Layer), whose every block opens with a 1M-gas L1-info deposit: a limit of the whole block is never included. The block gas limit is read on its own, optional (a node that does not serve it never fails a quote), re-read every 10 minutes, and waited for at most 1.5 s after the read began and never past the caller's abort (then the known caps alone). Arbitrum One and Robinhood Chain execute at most 32M gas per transaction (`ArbGasInfo.getMaxTxGasLimit()`, read 2026-11-26): a need above it is refused, the limit is never clamped to it.
- RPC: a revert (code 3, "execution reverted", out of gas and other EVM errors, revert data whose message names no rate limit or node fault) is still "no candidate"; a per-item rate limit (`-32005`, `-32007`, `429`, or its text), node error or missing answer (an item left out, one with no `result`, an `eth_call` answering `null`, an error sent without an id), and a batch refused with a JSON-RPC error, are retried (after 300–600 ms, then 1–1.5 s) and then refused as `rate_limited` / `upstream_unavailable`. `error.data` is kept; URLs are taken out of node and transport error text. Batches stay at 10 calls, each bounded at 12 s per endpoint (also under the caller's signal).
- `rpcCallsPerSecond` (opt-in, unset by default): at most that many calls to one endpoint in any second, across every quote of the provider; batches carry at most that many calls and wait for their turn, and one that would wait more than 4 s goes to the next endpoint, or is refused as `rate_limited` — never queued without bound. Unset, nothing is limited, as in 0.4.11.
- Monad 62,000 MON → AUSD (the cp0x trigger case), recorded at block 125,409,523: unset, 1.130999 AUSD at impact 9981 as before; at `maxPriceImpactBps: 500`, refused "loses 99.8% …".
