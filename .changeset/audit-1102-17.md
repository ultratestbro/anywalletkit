`describeProtocolBehaviour` now applies `callTargets` to calls on chains that tx-preview does not read, such as Tron or TON. A source call into a contract that is not declared (`callTargets`, the spender, the source token, the deposit address or an own address) fails the suite, with or without native value, as it already did on EVM. Before, such a call only had to satisfy the fixture's `decode`. Module authors with a Tron or TON `call` plan must list its contract in `callTargets`. The suite's own tests now also cover unreadable calldata, unparsed permits and typed orders.
