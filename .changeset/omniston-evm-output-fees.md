The EVM destination-escrow validator now binds the escrow's fee split and timelocks before the HTLC secret is released (scan F12). `createOmnistonEvmOutputValidator` decodes `immutables.parameters` (protocolFee, integratorFee, protocolFeeRecipient, integratorFeeRecipient) and requires the fees to equal the quote's `protocolFeeUnits` / `integratorFeeUnits` exactly, `amount − fees` to equal `outputUnits`, a non-zero protocol fee to go to the quote's `settlementData.protocolFeeRecipientAddress` and a non-zero integrator fee to the quote's integrator; it pins the destination timelock stages (words 4–6) to 1/120/180 s (source stages stay free: TON-source orders carry 0/0/0/0) and refuses disclosure within 45 s of the destination cancellation stage. `EvmOutputValidationInput` and the `validateEvmOutputPosition` hook gain `protocolFeeRecipientAddress`, which the provider passes from the cached quote; a non-zero fee without its recipient in the quote is refused (`quote-missing-protocol-fee-recipient`, `quote-missing-integrator-address`). The validator accepts `onRefusal` and names every refusal (`fee-parameters-shape`, `fee-amounts`, `protocol-fee-recipient`, `integrator-fee-recipient`, `dst-timelocks`, `dst-cancellation-window`, …), like the EVM input validator. Fixtures: ten real destination escrows on Base, Arbitrum, BNB and Polygon.
