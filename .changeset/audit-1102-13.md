`eth_sendTransaction`: when the network's fees fell to less than a quarter of the cap the review sheet showed (a fee spike passed while the sheet was open), the transaction is now signed at the current fees (2 × base fee + tip, or the current gas price) instead of the stale cap, without a second sheet — the user pays at most what they approved. Before, a provider-backed signer (a `WalletAccountEvm` with a `provider`, which refuses caller-set fees far above the network quote) could refuse the approved transaction after the user said yes. A rise in fees still asks again, as before.
